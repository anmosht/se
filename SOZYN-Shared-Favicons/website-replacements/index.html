Company Overview

SOZYN LLP is an Indian technology company focused on modernizing employment intelligence, workforce verification, and digital hiring solutions. Established with the vision of building trust between employers and professionals, SOZYN develops secure platforms that enable organizations to make informed employment decisions while empowering individuals to maintain complete ownership of their professional records.

The traditional recruitment process often requires employers to review hundreds of resumes, manually verify employment history, collect supporting documents, and conduct extensive background verification before making a hiring decision. These activities consume valuable time, increase recruitment costs, and frequently delay onboarding. SOZYN was created to simplify this process through technology-driven verification workflows, standardized employment records, and secure document management.

Our solutions are designed around a simple principle: employment information should be transparent, authenticated where possible, securely managed, and shared only with the individual's consent. Rather than requiring professionals to repeatedly submit the same employment information for every job application, SOZYN enables them to maintain a continuously updated professional profile throughout their career.

Our Platforms

SOZYN delivers multiple integrated platforms that work together to create a trusted employment ecosystem.

  • JobSeekerBoard – A dedicated platform for students, job seekers, and working professionals to maintain verified employment history, educational qualifications, certifications, career achievements, and employment-related documents.
  • Employer Suite – A comprehensive portal for organizations to request candidate authorization, review verified employment information, manage employment verification workflows, and make informed recruitment decisions.
  • MoonSpy – An employment intelligence solution developed to assist organizations in identifying potential dual employment scenarios and promoting workplace transparency while respecting privacy and applicable regulations.

What We Do

SOZYN provides technology solutions that enable candidates to securely manage their complete professional journey in one centralized platform. Users can maintain detailed employment records, academic qualifications, professional certifications, employment timelines, salary information, project experience, and employment-related documentation throughout their career.

Organizations, after obtaining the candidate's explicit authorization, can review verified employment information to support recruitment, employment verification, workforce planning, and talent acquisition. This consent-based model helps protect candidate privacy while reducing repetitive documentation and improving recruitment efficiency.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a trusted employment ecosystem where candidates maintain lifelong ownership of their professional records, organizations gain access to reliable employment information through candidate consent, and recruitment processes become faster, more transparent, and more efficient for everyone involved.

Our Vision

We envision a future where employment verification is no longer a repetitive administrative task but a secure, standardized, and digitally managed process. Through continuous innovation, SOZYN aims to become one of India's most trusted employment intelligence platforms, helping employers recruit with greater confidence while enabling professionals to build verified digital career identities.

Privacy & Candidate Control

Privacy is one of the core principles of every SOZYN platform. Candidates remain the owners of their information and decide when, how, and with whom their professional records are shared. Organizations cannot access employment records, supporting documents, or verification information without the candidate's approval, and access can be revoked by the candidate whenever permitted by platform policies.

Our Commitment

At SOZYN, we are committed to delivering secure, reliable, and technology-driven employment solutions that simplify hiring while maintaining transparency, accountability, and trust. We continuously invest in improving our platforms to meet the evolving needs of candidates, employers, educational institutions, and organizations across India.

Founder's Message

Dear Job Seekers, Employers, Partners, and Visitors,


Every company has a story. SOZYN began with mine.


Before becoming an entrepreneur, I was an employee. Like many professionals, I started my career with dreams of building a successful future. However, my journey was far from perfect. During the early years of my career, I struggled to adapt to professional work culture, time management, workplace discipline, and the expectations that organizations place on their employees.


Looking back, I acknowledge that many of the challenges I faced were the result of my own decisions. I preferred flexibility over structure, underestimated the importance of consistency, and did not always appreciate how attendance, communication, accountability, and workplace professionalism contribute to long-term career success.


Those decisions affected my employment journey. Some opportunities ended earlier than expected, while others were lost before they could truly begin. Every setback became a lesson that taught me something new about responsibility, professionalism, and the importance of building trust with employers.


As time passed, I realized that employment is much more than earning a salary. It is the foundation upon which individuals build their careers, support their families, earn respect, and create opportunities for the future. Losing employment can impact not only financial stability but also confidence, personal growth, and overall quality of life.


During my own job search, I experienced another challenge. Every new employer wanted to verify my previous employment history. I often wondered why candidates had to repeatedly prove the same employment records to every organization they joined. I realized that employment verification was often fragmented, time-consuming, and inconsistent for both employers and candidates.


That realization inspired the creation of SOZYN.


My vision was not simply to build another employment platform. I wanted to create an ecosystem where professionals could maintain a lifelong employment profile, preserve their career achievements, securely store employment records, and share verified information with organizations only through their consent.


Through JobSeekerBoard, candidates can maintain their employment history, education, certifications, and career documents in one secure place. Through Employer Suite, organizations can review verified employment information after receiving authorization from the candidate, helping simplify recruitment and employment verification.


My hope is that JobSeekerBoard encourages professionals to value honesty, accountability, discipline, and continuous improvement throughout their careers. A transparent employment history not only builds confidence with employers but also motivates individuals to maintain strong professional standards wherever they work.


SOZYN is not about judging a person's past. It is about helping individuals build a stronger future. Everyone makes mistakes. What truly matters is learning from those experiences, improving ourselves, and creating opportunities for growth.


If my own career journey—both its successes and its failures—can inspire even one professional to protect their employment, maintain a strong work ethic, and build a trusted career record, then the purpose behind SOZYN has already begun to succeed.


Thank you for taking the time to learn about our journey. I sincerely hope SOZYN becomes a platform that helps professionals and organizations build stronger, more transparent, and more trustworthy employment relationships.



With gratitude,


Mohammad Shahid
Founder & Managing Partner
SOZYN LLP

1. Nature of Services

SOZYN operates a specialized digital ecosystem that bridges pre-verified job seekers with prospective employers. The platform functions as both a niche aircraft maintenance and technical talent board and a background verification (BGV) suite. Our services facilitate candidate-driven credential submission and employer-driven validation, performance feedback, and authenticity mapping.


2. Candidate Account Sovereignty & Data Accuracy

  • The Solo Right to Edit: Candidates retain complete, exclusive autonomy over their personal profiles. You hold the absolute solo right to update, modify, or edit your uploaded information—including personal details, educational history, employment milestones, and professional certifications—at any time.
  • Purpose of Modification: Profile editing features are provided strictly to maintain data accuracy for legitimate vetting and background screening purposes.
  • Verification Integrity: Once information is locked for an active verification check, system-generated verification statuses, comments, or official remarks provided by verifying authorities cannot be altered or bypassed by the candidate.

3. Restrictive Access, Paywalls, and Employer Compliance

  • Data Shielding: To guarantee utmost privacy, candidate data portfolios are entirely shielded from public indexing, scrapers, and unauthenticated web traffic.
  • Minimal Premium Access Fee: No employer or third-party entity can view, access, or download a candidate’s granular personal, educational, or employment history unless a mandatory, minimal processing payment is successfully processed through our designated payment gateway.
  • Authorized Use: Information unlocked via payment must be used solely for internal corporate hiring evaluation, verification checks, and genuine talent assessment. Redistribution or commercial resale of candidate profiles is strictly prohibited.

4. Acceptable Use & Professional Confidentiality

Users agree not to misuse the platform by uploading fraudulent certifications, impersonating individuals, or deploying malicious scripts. In alignment with professional technical standards, all data processed on this platform is treated with elite operational loyalty and strict technical confidentiality.


5. Limitation of Liability

SOZYN acts as an intermediary secure data infrastructure. While we enforce rigid data verification checks, the ultimate hiring decisions, employment contractual obligations, and independent background conclusions remain the sole responsibility of the interacting Candidate and Employer.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026


At SOZYN, we respect your data privacy and are fiercely committed to protecting your personally identifiable information (PII). This Privacy Policy outlines how our web portal collects, structures, secures, and controls access to your data portfolio.


1. Information Architecture (What We Collect)

To deliver a highly functional pre-verified background screening suite, we collect the following structured information directly from Candidates:

  • Identity & Personal Data: Full legal name, contact channels (email, phone number), and location parameters.
  • Employment History: Granular details of previous employment tenures, exact corporate roles, operational backgrounds, and structural references.
  • Academic & Professional Credentials: Degrees, academic institute details, technical licenses, and professional certifications.

2. Data Isolation & Purpose-Driven Processing

Your data is processed strictly for verification, credential validation, and background screening purposes. We do not engage in behavioral advertisement targeting, data-broker leasing, or public profile indexing. Your data remains isolated within our encrypted database environment until explicitly queried by a legitimate stakeholder.


3. The Paywall Access Model

To prevent bulk corporate data harvesting and maintain deep candidate security, your detailed profile is gated behind a payment wall. Only verified Employers who have completed a minimal transactional payment gain access to view your full history, uploaded certificates, and associated verification comments.


4. Candidate Rights & Data Sovereignty

In compliance with modern data principles, you have full control over your footprint:

  • Right to Rectification: You maintain continuous, solo user rights to modify or correct inaccurate fields within your professional asset pool.
  • Data Minimization: When background comments or validation metrics are applied, only the necessary factual conclusions are surfaced to paid evaluators, maintaining clear privacy boundaries.

5. Information Security Controls

We deploy robust digital security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of your uploaded documentation. Access logs are strictly monitored, ensuring that who viewed your profile, when it was unlocked, and the purpose of the query are tracked and secure.
